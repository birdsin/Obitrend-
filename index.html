AI-assisted fashion concept creation

Explore fashion ideas from the clothes you already have.

OBITREND AI Fashion Creator helps fashion sellers, designers, stylists and clothing enthusiasts explore visual concepts using garment photos. Start with a clothing reference, choose available creative options, and review the generated image before using it in your project.

What you can explore

Garment-led concepts

Use a clothing photo as a visual reference. Check the result for differences in colour, fit, print, seams and other garment details.

Styling ideas

Explore visual presentation options and fashion settings available in the creator to develop a direction for your project.

Campaign inspiration

Create starting points for mood boards, social content, product presentation and fashion campaign planning.

How it works

1. Choose a photo

Upload a clear image of clothing you own or have permission to use.

2. Select options

Choose the available creative options that fit the fashion concept you want to explore.

3. Generate and review

Inspect the generated image and only use it when it meets your needs and respects applicable rights and rules.

Responsible use and AI limitations

AI outputs may alter garment details, colours, fit, people or backgrounds. OBITREND does not guarantee exact reproduction or that every image is suitable for commercial use. Review outputs before representing them as real products. Upload only material you are authorised to use, and do not use the service for unlawful, deceptive or infringing activity.

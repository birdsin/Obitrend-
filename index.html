🔒 Privacy & Account

Review how OBITREND handles account information, uploaded clothing references, generated images, payments and account security.

Your Privacy Matters

Account Information
Your email address is used to create and manage your OBITREND account and authenticate your secure session.

Uploaded Clothing Images
Uploaded clothing images are used as references for the fashion images you request.

Generated Images
Recent creations may be stored locally in this browser so they can appear in Gallery & History.

Payments
Pro payments are processed through the configured payment provider. Payment card information is not stored directly in the OBITREND interface.

Biometric / Passkey Security
When enabled, passkeys use the device's secure authenticator. OBITREND does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data. The authenticator keeps the private credential on the device or credential provider.

Account Deletion
You can permanently delete your authenticated OBITREND account from this Account overlay. Deletion is performed through the secure server-side account deletion endpoint.

Policy Updates
This policy may be updated when OBITREND introduces new features or changes how the service operates.

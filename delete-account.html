Delete Your OBITREND Account

If you would like to delete your OBITREND account and associated personal data, you can request account deletion from OBITREND.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once your deletion request has been processed, your account and eligible associated data may no longer be recoverable.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your OBITREND account, contact OBITREND support using the contact method provided below.

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your OBITREND account.
  • Your request to permanently delete your account.
  • Any information needed to verify that you own the account.

What Happens After Your Request

  • Your request will be reviewed.
  • We may verify account ownership for security purposes.
  • Your account will be deleted after verification.
  • Some information may be retained where required by law, security requirements, or legitimate business purposes.

Data Deletion

When an account deletion request is completed, OBITREND will delete or anonymize personal data associated with the account where applicable, subject to legal and operational requirements.

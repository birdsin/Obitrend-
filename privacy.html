Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how OBITREND AI Fashion Creator ("OBITREND", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website and application.

OBITREND is an AI-powered fashion creation service that allows users to upload images and create fashion-related images using artificial intelligence.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use OBITREND, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: information you provide when creating or managing an account, such as your email address and authentication information.
  • Uploaded images: images that you voluntarily upload for fashion creation and image-generation features.
  • Generated images: images created through the OBITREND AI generation service.
  • Usage information: information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Payment information: payment-related information required to process subscriptions or upgrades. Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. OBITREND does not intentionally store complete payment card numbers or card security codes on its own servers.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide AI fashion image-generation features.
  • Process images submitted by users.
  • Provide generated images and related features.
  • Process subscriptions, upgrades, and payments through payment providers.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and security of OBITREND.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and misuse of the service.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. AI Image Processing

OBITREND processes images submitted by users in order to provide its AI fashion creation features.

Users should only upload images that they own or have permission to use. Users are responsible for ensuring that their uploaded content does not violate another person's rights or applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Services

OBITREND may use trusted third-party services to provide functions such as authentication, database services, AI processing, hosting, analytics, security, and payment processing.

These service providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to OBITREND and its users, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Uploaded and generated images may be retained for features such as image history where the service provides such functionality. Users may delete available content through the controls provided by the application where applicable.

8. Account and Data Deletion

If you have an OBITREND account, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Account deletion requests may be submitted through the account deletion functionality provided within OBITREND or by contacting us using the support contact below.

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other lawful purposes.

OBITREND is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. We do not knowingly request personal information from children where such collection is prohibited.

You are responsible for the content you upload to OBITREND. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to use or content that violates applicable laws or the rights of others.

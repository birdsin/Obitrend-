Product Photography

Plan clean, polished product imagery for a fashion store, with attention to clear presentation and useful product views.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Prepare a list of views customers need: front, back, side, detail and, where appropriate, a genuine model photograph. Choose an uncluttered background and consistent lighting. Keep the item fully visible and record verified product information separately.

What to review before using the result

For commerce, use genuine photographs of the actual item as the factual reference. AI-generated product visuals can alter colour, details and construction. Do not use them as the only evidence of what a buyer will receive.

Practical workflow and example

Plan clear, truthful views that help customers understand a real item. Create a repeatable setup: neutral background, consistent light, stable camera position and a shot list covering front, back, side and detail views. Keep the garment clean, arranged and fully in frame. Record measurements separately.

For trousers, photograph the front, back, waistband, closure, pockets and hem. Use consistent lighting for each colourway so customers can compare them.

Quality-control checklist

Use genuine photographs for factual product representation. AI visuals may support campaign concepts but can alter colour, fabric, fit and construction.

  • Compare the result with the original reference or verified product information.
  • Inspect the full-size image and a mobile preview.
  • Keep the original upload and approved version for comparison.
  • Use only material you own or have permission to use.
Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

Fashion Campaigns

Develop a cohesive creative direction for a collection launch, promotion or brand story rather than treating each image as an unrelated idea.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Write one campaign objective, identify the audience, select a mood and setting, and list the essential images. Keep model, background and camera direction consistent across the campaign. Plan a hero image plus supporting views and detail shots.

What to review before using the result

Review the images as a set. Look for consistent styling and lighting, but also verify that the garment remains recognisable in every image. Do not imply a real event, endorsement or product feature that does not exist.

Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

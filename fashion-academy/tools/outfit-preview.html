Outfit Preview

Plan a complete outfit concept around a reference garment, considering complementary pieces and overall styling.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Identify the reference item first. Describe the occasion and the look you want, then choose complementary clothing and footwear that do not compete with the main piece. Be clear about which item must remain the visual focus.

What to review before using the result

Check that the added styling does not cover or change the reference garment. Generated combinations are inspiration; confirm that every accessory and companion item is actually available before presenting an outfit as purchasable.

Practical workflow and example

Explore styling combinations around a reference garment. Treat the uploaded garment as the anchor. Choose complementary pieces based on occasion, colour balance, proportion and season. Limit the number of added items so the main garment remains easy to identify. Decide which accessories are optional and which are essential to the concept.

A plain white shirt might be styled with tailored trousers and simple shoes for work, or denim and a light jacket for a casual look. Keep the shirt itself unchanged when the goal is to show styling options.

Quality-control checklist

Check that added layers do not cover important construction details and that the result does not imply included accessories are part of the product being sold.

  • Compare the result with the original reference or verified product information.
  • Check the image at full size and on a mobile screen.
  • Keep the original upload and final approved version for comparison.
  • Use only images and reference material you have permission to use.
Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

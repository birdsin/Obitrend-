All 20 OBITREND Creator Studio tools explained

A practical guide to the twenty creative tools available in the OBITREND Creator Studio. Each guide explains what the tool is intended to help with, how to prepare a useful brief and what to check before using the result.

These guides describe creative workflows and review considerations. They do not promise that every tool produces a final photograph, removes every background perfectly or verifies a physical product. Check the options shown in your current Studio account.

  1. 01. Fashion Image Creator

    Turn a clear garment reference and a defined creative direction into an editorial-style fashion concept. Use this tool when you have a specific visual idea but need to organise the mood, styling and presentation before creating.

  2. 02. AI Model Selection

    Plan the type of model presentation that suits a collection and its intended audience. The available model directions include female, male, androgynous and model-duo concepts.

  3. 03. Pose Studio

    Explore poses and movement for a fashion image, from a straightforward standing view to walking, seated or dynamic directions.

  4. 04. Background Studio

    Choose a visual setting that supports the garment and campaign rather than competing with it. Available directions include studio, architecture, nature and city settings.

  5. 05. Colour Variations

    Explore alternative colour directions for a garment concept, including tonal, contrasting, seasonal or custom palettes.

  6. 06. Fashion Campaigns

    Develop a cohesive creative direction for a collection launch, promotion or brand story rather than treating each image as an unrelated idea.

  7. 07. Outfit Preview

    Plan a complete outfit concept around a reference garment, considering complementary pieces and overall styling.

  8. 08. Virtual Try-On

    Prepare a visual concept showing how a garment might be presented on a model. It can help explore styling and presentation directions.

  9. 09. Sketch to Fashion

    Develop a fashion sketch into a more complete visual direction for a finished-look concept.

  10. 10. Fabric & Texture

    Explore fabric and surface-texture directions that may suit a garment design, such as linen, satin, tweed or denim.

  11. 11. Pattern Creator

    Develop repeat-print and surface-design ideas for clothing, from motif direction to an overall pattern concept.

  12. 12. Accessory Styling

    Explore accessories that complement a clothing look, including bags, shoes and other styling details.

  13. 13. Product Photography

    Plan clean, polished product imagery for a fashion store, with attention to clear presentation and useful product views.

  14. 14. Lookbook Creator

    Art-direct a coordinated set of looks so a collection reads as one considered visual story.

  15. 15. Social Content

    Plan fashion-led visuals for social channels, taking account of the message, audience and placement.

  16. 16. Editorial Studio

    Create a distinctive mood for a magazine-style fashion story through styling, lighting, pose and composition.

  17. 17. Image Enhancer

    Plan refinements to an existing fashion image, with the goal of improving presentation while retaining truthful details.

  18. 18. Background Remover

    Prepare a clean product-isolation direction for placing a garment or accessory against a simpler background.

  19. 19. Fashion Moodboard

    Bring visual references together to communicate a collection's mood, palette, textures and styling direction.

  20. 20. Collection Planner

    Explore a consistent design direction across a capsule collection, helping individual pieces feel related.

Accuracy first: AI-generated fashion visuals can alter colour, fit, texture and construction. Use genuine photographs and verified specifications when customers need to understand an actual product.

Virtual Try-On

Prepare a visual concept showing how a garment might be presented on a model. It can help explore styling and presentation directions.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Use a clear, front-facing reference where possible. Describe the intended model presentation and pose, and note garment details that matter, such as length, neckline, sleeve shape or fastening. Keep the brief focused on the actual item.

What to review before using the result

Virtual try-on images can alter fit, body proportions, drape and garment construction. They are not a reliable size recommendation or proof of how an item will fit a customer. Provide real measurements and fit guidance separately.

Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

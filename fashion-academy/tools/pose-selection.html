OBITREND Creator Studio · Tool guide

Pose Studio

Explore poses and movement for a fashion image, from a straightforward standing view to walking, seated or dynamic directions.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Choose a pose that makes the garment easy to inspect. Use a front-facing or three-quarter pose for shape and construction, a walking pose for movement, or a seated pose only when it does not hide important garment details. Describe hand placement and body orientation where useful.

What to review before using the result

Check that the pose does not obscure the neckline, waist, sleeves, pockets, hem or other defining details. Inspect hands and limbs for common image-generation errors.

Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

Pattern Creator

Develop repeat-print and surface-design ideas for clothing, from motif direction to an overall pattern concept.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Describe the motif, scale, repeat style, spacing and colour palette. Consider how the design will appear across seams and garment panels. If the pattern is for a particular item, specify the placement and whether the repeat should be subtle or prominent.

What to review before using the result

Inspect repeats for visible seams, awkward joins and inconsistent scale. Check that the pattern does not unintentionally resemble protected artwork or a third-party brand mark. Confirm print feasibility with a production sample.

Practical workflow and example

Plan repeat patterns that are coherent, legible and appropriate for the garment. Choose a motif, repeat scale, spacing and limited palette. Decide whether the repeat should be regular, staggered, directional or scattered. Preview the pattern at close range and at the scale it would appear on the finished garment.

A small geometric repeat may suit a shirt, while a large motif may need careful placement around seams and pockets. Consider how the repeat aligns across panels before production.

Quality-control checklist

Check for broken repeats, awkward joins, unintended symbols and distortion across seams. Confirm rights to reference motifs and obtain a production swatch.

  • Compare the result with the original reference or verified product information.
  • Inspect the full-size image and a mobile preview.
  • Keep the original upload and approved version for comparison.
  • Use only material you own or have permission to use.
Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

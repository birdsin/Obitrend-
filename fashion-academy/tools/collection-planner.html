Collection Planner

Explore a consistent design direction across a capsule collection, helping individual pieces feel related.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Define the collection's audience, purpose and season. Choose a limited palette, identify recurring silhouettes or details, and decide how each piece contributes something distinct. Consider how items can work together without becoming repetitive.

What to review before using the result

Treat the plan as an early creative framework. Validate materials, costing, sizing, supplier capacity and production feasibility independently before committing to manufacture or advertising.

Practical workflow and example

Organise a collection so each item has a role and the range feels coherent. Define the audience, season, price position and collection purpose. Build a range plan listing garment category, colour family, key material, size range and how each piece relates to others. Identify gaps and duplication before sampling.

A small capsule might include one outer layer, two tops, one bottom and one versatile dress. A shared palette connects the pieces while different silhouettes give customers meaningful choices.

Quality-control checklist

Treat the plan as a creative framework, not a production forecast. Validate supplier capacity, costing, fit, sizing, material availability and demand independently.

  • Compare the result with the original reference or verified product information.
  • Inspect the full-size image and a mobile preview.
  • Keep the original upload and approved version for comparison.
  • Use only material you own or have permission to use.
Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

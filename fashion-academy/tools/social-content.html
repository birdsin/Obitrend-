OBITREND Creator Studio · Tool guide

Social Content

Plan fashion-led visuals for social channels, taking account of the message, audience and placement.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Choose the platform and purpose first: launch announcement, styling tip, collection overview or behind-the-scenes concept. Select an aspect ratio suited to the placement, keep key details away from crop edges and write a short caption that adds useful context.

What to review before using the result

Check the final crop on a phone. Make sure text is readable, garment details remain visible and any AI-generated image is not presented as an unaltered documentary photograph.

Practical workflow and example

Adapt fashion imagery to a platform and audience without losing product clarity. Decide the platform, audience and single message before choosing a crop. Keep garment details away from text overlays and interface edges. Write a caption that adds useful context rather than repeating the image.

A product launch post can introduce the item and key verified details; a story sequence can show front, back and close-up views. Avoid squeezing everything into one graphic.

Quality-control checklist

Preview on a phone. Check readability, contrast, safe areas, spelling, image rights and factual accuracy.

  • Compare the result with the original reference or verified product information.
  • Inspect the full-size image and a mobile preview.
  • Keep the original upload and approved version for comparison.
  • Use only material you own or have permission to use.
Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

OBITREND Creator Studio · Tool guide

Colour Variations

Explore alternative colour directions for a garment concept, including tonal, contrasting, seasonal or custom palettes.

How to use this tool thoughtfully

Decide whether you are exploring a hypothetical design option or trying to represent an existing item. Name the colours clearly and consider how they work with the fabric, print and intended collection. Keep a note of the original colour for comparison.

What to review before using the result

This tool's colour concepts are not evidence that a physical product exists in those colours. Label proposed colourways as concepts and verify dye, fabric and production samples before advertising availability.

Practical workflow and example

Explore design colour ideas without confusing concepts with actual product stock. First decide whether you are exploring a proposed redesign or documenting an existing item. For a concept, define a small palette and compare light, dark and accent options. For an existing product, preserve the real colour and do not publish a recoloured AI result as a factual product image.

A designer testing a future collection might compare navy, oxblood and sand as concept options. A retailer selling a specific navy garment should keep its product photography true to the actual colour.

Quality-control checklist

Check colour under neutral light and compare with a physical sample. Screen settings and image-generation outputs are not reliable colour measurement tools.

  • Compare the result with the original reference or verified product information.
  • Check the image at full size and on a mobile screen.
  • Keep the original upload and final approved version for comparison.
  • Use only images and reference material you have permission to use.
Important: Tool options and availability can change. A generated visual or creative brief is not a substitute for checking the real garment, product specifications, rights and customer-facing claims.

What these resources cover

Good fashion visuals begin before a camera or image generator is opened. A clear reference, a defined audience, a consistent visual story and an honest review process all help a creative project stay focused. Each guide explains the reason behind its recommendations and includes steps that can be applied to a small clothing business, an independent designer or a personal fashion project.

How to use AI-generated fashion visuals responsibly

AI images can help explore styling and campaign directions, but they can also change garment details. Treat generated visuals as concepts unless you have checked them against the real item. Do not use an AI interpretation as the sole evidence of colour, fabric, fit, construction or product condition. When customers are making a purchase decision, provide genuine item photographs and accurate written specifications.

Editorial standard: These guides are educational resources, not claims that every feature or generated result will behave identically for every image. Always review the output and verify product details independently.

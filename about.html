About OBITREND AI Fashion Creator

OBITREND is an AI-assisted fashion concept tool for people who want to explore how clothing might be presented in visual content. Users provide a clothing image, choose available creative options, and generate a fashion image for review.

Who it is for

The service may be useful to independent fashion sellers, designers, stylists, content creators and clothing enthusiasts who need visual inspiration or early-stage campaign concepts.

What it can and cannot do

AI outputs are generated interpretations, not verified photographs of a real product. Details such as colour, seams, patterns, fit, accessories, body proportions and backgrounds can differ from the reference. Review each result before using it to represent a product or making a customer promise.

Responsible use

Upload only material you own or are authorised to use. Do not use the service to infringe intellectual property, mislead customers, violate privacy or create prohibited content. Users are responsible for reviewing generated material and complying with applicable laws.

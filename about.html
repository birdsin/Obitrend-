About the product

About OBITREND AI Fashion Creator

OBITREND is an AI-assisted fashion creation service. It gives fashion sellers, designers, stylists and content creators a workspace for exploring how a clothing reference might be presented in campaign-style imagery and related visual concepts.

How the service works

A user brings a clothing image into the Creator Studio, selects from the options available in the interface and submits a generation request. The resulting image is an AI-generated interpretation for review. Available tools and options may change as the service develops.

Who may find it useful

Independent clothing businesses can use visual concepts when planning a collection or campaign. Designers and stylists can explore presentation directions. Content creators can develop early ideas for fashion stories. The service is intended to support creative exploration; it does not replace professional product photography, fit testing or garment inspection.

AI limitations and review

An AI-generated result is not proof of how a physical item looks. Colour, texture, construction, seams, prints, proportions, fit, accessories and background details may differ from the uploaded reference. Check every result carefully. For product listings, sizing claims, customer orders or other purchase decisions, use accurate product information and genuine photographs of the item.

Responsible use

Upload only images and other material that you own or have permission to use. Respect privacy, publicity rights, copyright and trademarks. Do not use the service to mislead customers or create unlawful or prohibited content. You are responsible for checking that your uploads and use of generated results are appropriate.

Independent learning resources

The OBITREND Fashion Academy provides practical guides on garment-photo preparation, campaign planning and responsible product presentation. The guides are public resources and do not require a Studio account.

Support and policies

For account, subscription or technical questions, contact OBITREND support. Read the Privacy Policy to understand data handling and the Terms of Use for service conditions.
